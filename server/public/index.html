ABOUT THE GAME

TactShoot is a fast, free-to-play first-person shooter for Android. Real-time shadows, dynamic skies, three handcrafted maps, five weapons and two game modes: Team Deathmatch and Free For All.

  • Play online against real players - quick play or private rooms
  • No account needed: pick a callsign and drop in
  • Share a 4-letter room code to squad up with friends
  • Offline practice mode with smart AI bots (3 difficulties)
  • Zero ads, zero pay-to-win. Just aim.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Install the TactShoot APK on any Android 7.0+ device
  • Open the game, set your callsign in Settings
  • Tap PLAY ONLINE, choose TDM or FFA
  • Or grab a room code from a friend and hit JOIN
  • Left thumb moves, right thumb aims, big button fires
  • Double-tap fire for auto-fire, tap ADS to zoom
